Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Crusher Compound does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Your Photo

If you use the optional “Your Photo” feature, you pick one picture with the system photo picker. The app receives only that picture — it has no access to the rest of your library — keeps a copy only on your device to show it in the game, and never uploads or shares it. Choosing another picture replaces it; deleting the app deletes it.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stones broken, crystals found, chosen stone, tool and place) and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

All purchases are one-time and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks Apple which purchases were made, to unlock content.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
