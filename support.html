Questions, ideas or a bug? Write to yungqifr@gmail.com — we answer within a few days.

I bought the Full Version but it is locked

Open Settings (the sliders icon) and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

No vibration

Check Settings in the app, and in iOS: Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics. iPad has no vibration motor.

No sound

The game respects the silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check the volume.
